## What to do

Find the payment in the GOV.UK Pay admin tool using its payment ID.

* If its status is 'success', refund it in full. Only refund the payment identified in this email. Other payments for the same registration may have been taken successfully.
* If its status is 'capturable', cancel it. Otherwise it will expire 120 hours after it was created.
* If it has any other status, you do not need to do anything.
